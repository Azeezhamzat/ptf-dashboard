📈 Diversity Prediction Theorem Analysis

The DPT Identity

E = ε - δ

  • E = Collective error = (μ - θ)²
  • ε = Mean individual error = mean((xᵢ - θ)²)
  • δ = Predictive diversity = variance

The B-L hypothesis: Anchoring increases both ε and δ, but Δδ > Δε → E decreases
